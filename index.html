CLINICAL UNDERSTANDING

What is the Problem?

Hair loss (alopecia) is a biological medical condition, not a cosmetic flaw or lack of hygiene.

Androgenetic Alopecia (Pattern Baldness)

The leading cause affecting both men (receding hairline, crown thinning) and women (widening center part). Genetically sensitized follicles shrink under DHT, producing thinner, shorter hairs until roots turn dormant.

Telogen Effluvium (Acute Shedding)

Sudden, diffuse hair fall triggered by severe fever (Dengue, Typhoid), postpartum stress, or iron, Vitamin D3, or B12 deficiencies pushing hundreds of hairs into resting phase.
